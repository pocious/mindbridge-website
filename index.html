03
The MindBridge Ecosystem
One ecosystem. Four products.
Built to work as one.
IRIS
Identity Infrastructure
One verified record, recognised across every institution and transaction in Uganda.
The identity spine of the MindBridge ecosystem — built on Bridge Chain for permanent, auditable verification across every institutional context.
Gavel
Legal Technology
Formalisation for Uganda's real economy — not just those who already know the system.
Smart document creation, digital signatures, and identity-verified contracts. Legal access for individuals and businesses at every level of formality.
Bridge Chain
Blockchain Infrastructure
Immutable records. Verifiable history. A foundation that institutions can trust.
Permissioned blockchain infrastructure that gives every transaction a permanent, tamper-proof record — the trust layer beneath everything MindBridge builds.
Smart SACCO
Cooperative Finance
Identity-first financial infrastructure for Uganda's cooperative sector.
Built on IRIS and Bridge Chain, Smart SACCO connects Uganda's informal savings groups to formal financial systems — without erasing what makes them work.
AI Research
Applied Intelligence
AI systems designed for African legal and institutional contexts — not adapted, built here.
Legal document intelligence, natural language processing for Luganda and English, and predictive analytics grounded in Uganda's real institutional landscape.
Governance
Digital Governance
Infrastructure for government agencies, courts, and public institutions.
Inter-agency data sharing, citizen record management, and transparent public-sector operations built on the same identity and trust infrastructure as every other MindBridge product.
